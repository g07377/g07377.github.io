01 / SCOPE & CONTACT

Scope and contact

This policy describes data handling for the Android app Daymotive (練日常), package life.muscle.beach. Its English and Traditional Chinese store titles are Daymotive: Workout Log and 練日常：健身菜單與訓練紀錄.

Developer / privacy contact: Natural Intelligence Studio

Email: testfornewin1@gmail.com

Contact us about privacy, data handling or deletion guidance. Correspondence you send will be handled through our email service to respond to your request. Avoid including unnecessary workout records, photos or other sensitive information.

02 / YOUR DATA

Data handled by the app

Fitness data comes mainly from what you enter, the workout actions and timing you initiate, and images or files you choose to import.

Workout and fitness records
Workout dates and time zones, start and end times, duration, exercises, sets, repetitions, training loads and units, completion or skip status, and notes. Cardio entries may include duration, distance, resistance and machine-reported calories. Training load is not a body-weight measurement.
Plans, exercises and equipment
Custom exercises and instructions, targets, rest periods, cycle order, equipment names, venue text and settings you enter, personal notes, and snapshots preserved when a workout begins.
Images you select
Exercise images and captions added through the system file picker, including images in imported packages. Images are copied into the app's storage. The current image workflow does not remove metadata that may already be embedded in the original file.
Preferences and operational records
Appearance, screen-on, rest alerts, workout notifications and assistant preferences; workout focus, countdown and assistant position. Record and revision identifiers, update times, deletion markers and image integrity information support storage, merging and recovery.
Advertising-related data
Google AdMob processes the data described in the advertising section below, separately from manually entered fitness content. The app also stores a local flag indicating whether content has been viewed, to help decide when to show an ad.
The fitness features do not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or wearable devices, or capture data from location, camera, microphone or body sensors. Text and images you select may still contain personal or health information. AdMob may infer approximate location from an IP address, as explained below.
03 / USE & STORAGE

Purposes and storage

Fitness content supports workout recording, plans, rest alerts, journals, trends, and the import, export, backup and recovery actions you request.

Workout records and preferences are primarily stored in the app's local database. Images and device state are stored in its private file space. The fitness workflow does not require an app account and does not provide automatic synchronization to a developer-operated account server. Advertising data is handled separately as described below.

The app configuration disables Android Auto Backup and excludes app data from cloud backup and device transfer rules. Files that you explicitly export or back up are separate from these rules.

Local storage relies on Android app isolation and device protections. The app does not add app-level database encryption or password encryption for backup files. Use a device lock and protect your exported files.

04 / EXPORT & BACKUP

Exports and cloud backups

You can choose a destination in the system file picker for text summaries, CSV files, plan files or full backups. A destination may be device storage, Google Drive or another installed document provider.

  • Text / CSV: workout information from the range you select, for reading or analysis.
  • Plan files: selected plans, exercise instructions, targets, related images and an optional cycle; these are not full workout-history backups.
  • Full backups: plans, personal text and images, equipment, cycles, workout snapshots, preferences, related images and deletion markers. They exclude Google account credentials, system permissions and running rest countdowns.
A full backup is an unencrypted ZIP. If you choose a cloud destination, its provider handles the file and may transmit and store it on its servers. Use your own account and appropriate access controls.

The app does not automatically publish backup links. The selected provider handles account access, synchronization, folder permissions and cloud retention under its own policies. A successful write in the app does not prove that cloud synchronization is complete; verify that with the provider.

05 / RETENTION & DELETION

Retention and deletion

No fixed number of days is currently set for automatic deletion of local workout records. You can edit or delete workout history, or archive supported content through the app.

Deleting workout history removes its detailed content and set entries, but retains deletion markers and some index information to prevent unintended restoration during a backup merge. This includes record identifiers, revision and deletion times, workout date, title and source plan identifier. Archived content and historical snapshots may remain.

Before merging or restoring, the app creates a local recovery copy. After a new copy is verified, it normally rotates these copies to keep the latest three. They may contain content that was later edited or deleted. Removing an image reference does not mean that its file or historical copies disappear immediately.

To remove all local app data, use Android's clear-storage function or uninstall the app. This affects your current workout records; decide first whether you need a backup.

Clearing app data does not delete exported files or cloud backups. Delete those separately in your file manager, Google Drive or the relevant service. Its trash, retention and other-copy rules apply. The app has no cloud-backup deletion interface.

Advertising data held by Google is subject to Google's retention rules and controls. Local deletion or an advertising-ID reset does not guarantee erasure of records already held by Google. We cannot delete all Google-held records on your behalf or promise a fixed retention period for them. Contact correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and applicable obligations, rather than for a specified fixed term.

06 / PERMISSIONS

Permissions and reminders

Notifications, vibration, scheduled alarms, foreground services and wake locks support user-started workouts and rest alerts. The optional assistant uses permission to display over other apps for a workout shortcut and countdown.

You can manage these preferences and authorizations in the app or Android settings. Disabling them may limit reminders or the assistant. Notifications and floating windows may reveal workout information on screen; adjust them and your lock-screen settings to suit your privacy needs.

The fitness features use a system file picker for files you select, without broad photo-library or storage access. They do not request location, camera or microphone access. AdMob uses network access and may use advertising identifiers according to its configuration and your device settings.

07 / ADVERTISING & CHOICES

Google AdMob and your choices

The app integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK for banner, interstitial and app-open ads. Google receives and processes advertising data under its own privacy policies.

The SDK automatically collects and shares data for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and device, app or account-related identifiers. Examples include an advertising ID, app set ID and, when enabled, publisher first-party ID. Available data depends on SDK settings, device controls and privacy choices. Processing can occur without an ad click.

Consent and advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and display applicable consent forms. SDK initialization and ad requests are controlled by the consent status reported by UMP. UMP communicates with Google to determine and present applicable privacy choices; available forms and choices depend on region and configuration.

When UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, you can reopen the form from Settings → Ad privacy options (「設定 → 廣告隱私選項」). This option is shown when applicable, not for every user at all times.

Android settings may let you reset or delete your advertising ID; availability and labels vary. Google also provides advertising controls and explanations. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve data processing and do not mean that no data is collected.

Fitness content stays separate from ad targeting

The app does not supply workout records, personal notes or photos as custom ad-targeting inputs. Workout-session state and the local content-viewed flag help decide when to show an ad; they do not supply the contents of your fitness records for ad targeting.

01 / 適用範圍與聯絡

適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策說明 Android 應用程式「練日常」（Daymotive）如何處理資料，套件名稱為 life.muscle.beach。繁中及英文商店標題分別為「練日常：健身菜單與訓練紀錄」及 Daymotive: Workout Log。

開發者／隱私聯絡名稱：Natural Intelligence Studio

電子郵件：testfornewin1@gmail.com

如需詢問隱私、資料處理或刪除方式，可透過上述信箱聯絡。你寄送的信件會透過我們使用的電子郵件服務處理，以回應你的請求。請避免附上不必要的訓練紀錄、照片或其他敏感資料。

02 / 處理的資料

App 處理哪些資料

健身資料主要來自你在 App 中輸入、操作與計時產生的訓練狀態，以及你主動選取匯入的圖片或檔案。

訓練與健身紀錄
訓練日期、時區、開始與結束時間、時長、動作、組數、次數、負重及單位、完成或跳過狀態、備註；有氧紀錄可包含時間、距離、阻力及器材顯示的熱量。負重欄位指訓練重量，並非體重測量。
菜單、動作與器材
自訂動作與說明、訓練目標、休息時間、循環順序、器材名稱、你填寫的場館文字與設定、個人筆記，以及訓練開始時保存的相關內容快照。
你選取的圖片
透過系統選檔器加入的動作圖片、圖說，以及匯入檔案內的相關圖片。圖片會複製至 App 儲存空間；目前圖片流程不會移除原始檔案可能已包含的中繼資料。
偏好與運作資料
外觀、螢幕常亮、休息提醒、訓練通知、小助手偏好，以及訓練位置、倒數、小助手位置。紀錄識別碼、修訂識別碼、更新時間、刪除標記及圖片檢核資訊用於保存、合併與還原。
廣告相關資料
Google AdMob 會處理下方廣告章節所述資料，與手動輸入的健身內容分開。App 另在本機保存是否已看過內容的旗標，用於判斷廣告展示時機。
健身功能沒有 Health Connect、Google Fit 或穿戴裝置資料串接，也沒有從定位、相機、麥克風或身體感測器擷取資料。你自行輸入的文字或選取的圖片仍可能包含個人或健康資訊。AdMob 可能透過 IP 推估概略位置，詳見廣告章節。
03 / 用途與保存

資料用途與保存位置

健身內容用於訓練記錄、菜單安排、休息提醒、日誌、統計趨勢，以及你要求的匯入、匯出、備份與還原。

訓練資料與偏好主要保存在 App 本機資料庫，圖片與裝置狀態保存在私有檔案空間。健身功能不需要建立 App 帳號，也不提供向開發者帳號伺服器的自動同步；廣告資料依下方說明分開處理。

App 設定停用 Android 自動備份，並在規則中排除 App 資料的雲端備份及裝置轉移。這與你自行匯出或建立的備份檔不同。

本機資料依賴 Android 的應用程式隔離與裝置保護機制；App 沒有另外提供 App 層級的資料庫加密或備份密碼加密。請使用裝置鎖定並妥善保管匯出檔案。

04 / 匯出與備份

匯出與雲端備份由你選擇

你可透過系統選檔器儲存文字摘要、CSV、菜單檔或完整備份。目的地可為手機儲存空間、Google Drive 或其他已安裝的文件提供者。

  • 文字摘要／CSV：包含你選定範圍的訓練內容，供閱讀或分析。
  • 菜單檔：包含選定菜單、動作教學、目標、相關圖片及可選循環，不作為完整訓練紀錄備份。
  • 完整備份：包含菜單、個人圖文、器材、循環、訓練快照、設定、相關圖片及刪除標記；不包含 Google 帳號憑證、系統權限或正在運行的休息倒數。
完整備份是未加密 ZIP。選擇雲端位置時，檔案會交給該服務處理，可能傳送及保存於其伺服器。請使用自己的帳號與適當存取權限。

App 不會自行公開備份連結。帳號存取、同步、資料夾權限與雲端保存由所選服務依其政策管理。App 寫入成功不代表雲端同步已完成，請在該服務確認。

05 / 保留與刪除

資料保留與刪除

目前沒有設定依固定天數自動刪除本機訓練紀錄的期限。你可在 App 提供的介面編輯、刪除歷史訓練，或封存支援的內容。

刪除歷史訓練會移除詳細內容與逐組資料，但為避免備份合併時無意恢復資料，仍保留刪除標記及部分索引資訊，包括紀錄識別碼、修訂與刪除時間、訓練日期、標題及來源菜單識別。封存內容與歷史快照可能繼續保留。

合併或整體還原前，App 會建立本機復原副本。新副本驗證完成後，通常輪替保留最近三份；這些副本可能含有後來已編輯或刪除的內容。移除圖片引用不代表圖片檔或歷史副本立即消失。

若要清除整個 App 的本機資料，可使用 Android 系統設定的清除儲存空間功能，或解除安裝 App。這會影響目前訓練紀錄，請先確認是否需要備份。

清除 App 資料不會刪除匯出檔案或雲端備份。請另至檔案管理工具、Google Drive 或相關服務刪除；垃圾桶、保留期限及其他副本依其規則處理。App 沒有雲端備份刪除入口。

Google 持有的廣告資料依 Google 的保留規則與控制方式處理。本機刪除或重設廣告識別碼，不保證已由 Google 保存的紀錄一併清除。我們無法代你刪除 Google 持有的所有紀錄，也不承諾其固定保留期限。聯絡信件則依處理請求及適用義務的需要保留，未訂定固定期間。

06 / 權限與提醒

系統權限與提醒

通知、震動、排程提醒、前景服務與喚醒鎖支援使用者啟動的訓練及休息提醒。可選小助手使用「顯示在其他應用程式上層」權限，提供返回訓練與倒數資訊。

你可在 App 或 Android 系統設定管理相關偏好與授權；關閉後部分提醒或小助手功能可能受限。訓練通知及浮動視窗可能顯示訓練資訊，請依隱私需求調整它們與鎖定畫面設定。

健身功能透過系統選檔器存取你選取的檔案，不要求廣泛相簿或儲存空間存取，也不要求定位、相機或麥克風權限。AdMob 使用網路，並可能依其設定與裝置設定使用廣告識別碼。

07 / 廣告與選擇

Google AdMob 與你的選擇

App 透過 Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK 整合 Google AdMob，提供橫幅、插頁及 App 開啟廣告。Google 依其隱私政策接收及處理廣告資料。

SDK 會自動收集及分享資料，用於廣告、成效衡量與分析，以及防範詐欺。資料包括 IP 位址（可推估概略位置）、App 與廣告互動、效能及診斷資訊，以及裝置、App 或帳號相關識別碼，例如廣告 ID、App set ID，以及啟用時的發布商第一方 ID。可取得的資料取決於 SDK 設定、裝置控制及隱私選擇；即使沒有點擊廣告，也可能發生資料處理。

同意與廣告選擇

App 使用 Google 的 User Messaging Platform（UMP）更新同意資訊，並顯示適用的同意表單。SDK 初始化與廣告請求依 UMP 回報的同意狀態控制。UMP 會與 Google 通訊，以判斷及呈現適用的隱私選擇；可用的表單與選擇依地區及設定而異。

當 UMP 判定需要提供隱私選項入口時，你可透過「設定 → 廣告隱私選項」（Settings → Ad privacy options）重新開啟表單。此選項於適用時顯示，並非所有使用者隨時都會看見。

Android 設定可能允許重設或刪除廣告識別碼，可用功能與名稱依版本而異。Google 亦提供廣告控制與說明。非個人化或受限廣告仍可能處理資料，不代表完全不收集資料。

健身內容與廣告定向分開

App 不會將訓練紀錄、個人筆記或照片提供作為自訂廣告定向輸入。訓練進行狀態及本機是否看過內容的旗標，用於判斷廣告展示時機，不會將健身紀錄內容提供作為廣告定向資料。
