1. About the App

Natural Intelligence Studio ("we", "us") develops Chatgpt秘笈(Prompt大全)-2026 ("the App"), Android package com.forlearn.gpt. The App may also display the names GPT祕笈, GPT 秘笈, or GPT Handbook in its launcher or interface. This policy applies to that App, not to every app produced by the developer.

Features and available controls can vary by app version.

2. Information stored on your device

The App uses its private local storage to provide its features:

  • Prompt library: custom prompt titles and text, categories, language, tags, favorites, usage counts, and update times.
  • Chat history: conversation titles, selected AI service and model, message roles and contents, and creation/update times.
  • Settings: interface language, service endpoint (Base URL), model, generation settings such as temperature, system prompt, migration status, and preferences such as ignored app-update versions.
  • API keys: credentials you enter for your selected AI services. The App encrypts these keys using Android Keystore-backed AES/GCM before saving them in its private storage.

Saving an item locally is different from sending it to an AI service or sharing it. Those transmissions occur through the features described below. Local storage can also be affected by Android backup and device-transfer behavior described in section 7.

3. AI requests and API keys

When you send a chat message, the App sends the message and the full message history of the current conversation, together with the selected model, generation settings, and any system prompt, to your selected AI endpoint. Your API key is sent to authenticate the request. The service returns a response, which the App stores in your local chat history. The service also receives network information such as your IP address.

The default services are OpenAI (api.openai.com) and Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). You can change the Base URL. If you choose a custom endpoint, the operator of that endpoint receives your API credential and request contents instead. Use only endpoints you trust.

The connection-test feature sends a fixed test message and your current service settings, API key, and system prompt if one is set. It does not send your chat history as part of that test.

Before sending: do not include information you do not want the selected provider to receive. The provider's own terms, account settings, and retention rules apply. OpenAI states that API content is not used for model training by default unless sharing is enabled, but it may retain content for abuse monitoring. Google's unpaid Gemini services may use inputs and outputs to improve products and may involve human review; paid-service rules differ. The App does not guarantee that a provider will never retain, review, or use your content.

See OpenAI API data controls and the Gemini API terms before using those services.

4. Advertising and consent

The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Advertising services process information to deliver and measure ads, understand performance, and prevent fraud. This may include:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device and advertising identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID where available;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views;
  • device/app information and diagnostics, such as operating system, SDK performance, and errors.

The App also includes the Meta Audience Network SDK and its AdMob mediation adapter. When Meta is used as an advertising source through mediation, Meta may process information associated with the ad request and ad interactions under its own privacy policy. Whether that source serves an ad depends on the mediation configuration and ad availability.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent information and show a consent form when applicable. Advertising SDK initialization can begin at app startup, before a consent form is displayed; a form should not be interpreted as a guarantee that no technical data processing has occurred beforehand. Ad requests are controlled using the consent status provided by UMP.

You can use the advertising-ID controls available in your Android privacy settings, and Google or Meta's own privacy controls. Resetting or deleting an advertising ID does not necessarily stop all operational, security, or non-personalized advertising data processing. The App does not currently provide a separate control to reopen the UMP privacy-options form.

Details: Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, Google partner-site and app information, and Meta Privacy Policy.

  • Open an AI website: when you choose to open a prompt in ChatGPT or Gemini, the prompt may be included in the destination URL. The destination service and browser receive it, and it may appear in browser history. Their account and privacy rules apply separately.
  • App updates: the App uses Google Play's in-app update service to check for and offer available updates. Google Play processes the related requests under Google's policies.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Do not send API keys, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Permissions and security

The App uses internet and network-state access for AI requests, advertising, and update-related functions. Its advertising components use advertising-related permissions. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permissions. This does not prevent online services from receiving network information or inferring an approximate location from an IP address.

Default AI endpoints use HTTPS. The App permits a local loopback connection to 127.0.0.1 without transport encryption for a locally configured service. Security and handling by a custom endpoint depend on that endpoint; do not assume it has the protections of the default providers.

API keys are encrypted in app-private storage using Android Keystore. The prompt and chat database does not have an additional app-level database-encryption layer. Protect your device and any backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention, deletion, and backup

Information on your device

The App does not apply a fixed automatic expiration period to your prompts, chat history, settings, or saved API keys. They remain in local storage until removed through the relevant controls or through Android's app-data management, subject to backup and legacy-data exceptions below.

  • Use the App's controls to delete a custom prompt, an individual conversation, or all local chat history. Deleting a conversation also removes its locally stored messages.
  • Use the API settings to delete the locally saved key for the selected service. This does not revoke the key at the provider. Revoke or rotate it through the provider's account controls if necessary.
  • To remove the App's stored data from the device, use Android Settings → Apps → this App → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data (labels vary by device). This resets the App's local data and settings. Uninstalling normally removes app-local storage, but does not remove separately retained backups or third-party copies.

Android backup and older notes

Current backup rules exclude the main prompt/chat database, current settings store, and encrypted API-key store from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Some other preferences and legacy notes from older app versions are not excluded and may be backed up depending on your system settings.

When older notes are migrated into the current prompt library, a legacy copy may remain in the App's older storage. Deleting the migrated prompt alone may not remove that copy. Android's Clear storage / Clear data removes the App's stored copies on that device; use your device or backup provider's controls to manage any separately retained backups. Previously shared content, clipboard contents, and browser history are also managed separately.

Information already sent elsewhere

Deleting local data does not request deletion from OpenAI, Google, a custom AI endpoint, an advertising provider, or an app or website you shared with. Those services retain information according to their policies and your account settings; use their privacy/account controls or contact them to request deletion. We do not set or guarantee their retention periods.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request, keep necessary support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to us. We cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device or directly control data held in your own third-party accounts.
