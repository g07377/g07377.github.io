About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Small Shop POS System, also listed in Traditional Chinese as 小店點餐系統, developed by Natural Intelligence Studio. The Android package name is gundon.gu.posystem. The App may display 點餐管理系統 on the device. These names refer to the same tablet POS application.

The App helps shops manage products, orders, inventory, accounting and reports. It does not require a separate account with us. Google Play handles the Google account used for subscriptions. Features and backup behavior can differ between installed versions.

Shop records and information on your device

The App stores shop information in its local database and settings. This includes products, recipes, categories, prices, inventory and costs, orders and customizations, table references and notes, sales and accounting entries, payment amounts and categories, reports, language and currency settings, trial status and subscription entitlement information. Versions with drafts, refunds and audit features also store those records and their history.

These shop records are used to operate the POS and generate reports. The App does not automatically upload the shop database to our subscription backend or send its contents as advertising parameters. Payment categories such as cash, card or mobile payment are records entered by the shop; the POS does not process customer card payments or request customer card numbers.

Information that you type in a note or other free-text field becomes part of your shop records. Avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information about customers or employees, and handle any information you enter in accordance with your own obligations.

Subscriptions and verification

When you buy, restore or check a subscription, Google Play processes the purchase and payment. The App sends the package name, subscription product identifier, purchase token and, where applicable, plan identifier over HTTPS to our Google Firebase / Google Cloud Functions backend to verify your entitlement with the Google Play Developer API.

The backend stores subscription verification records in Cloud Firestore. These can include the purchase token, product and plan, subscription state, start and expiry times, renewal and acknowledgement status, linked purchase references, verification times and notification history. Device or obfuscated account identifiers may be present if provided during verification or returned by Google Play. The Android App does not send your shop database with these requests.

We use this information to confirm purchases, restore access, handle renewals and cancellations, prevent misuse and resolve subscription problems. Google and its infrastructure providers may also process IP addresses and technical request or service logs. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play. Deleting the App or its data does not cancel a subscription; manage cancellation in Google Play.

Advertising

Versions distributed with advertising use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses these data for advertising delivery and personalization where permitted, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation.

Ads and their data processing are separate from the shop records kept in the local POS database. The App does not attach your orders, inventory, revenue, accounting notes or customer information to ad requests. Subscription entitlement can hide the App's banner ads.

You can manage available advertising controls in Android and your Google account. The choices and controls offered by Google depend on your device, region and settings. We do not claim that the App currently provides an in-app consent or advertising-privacy options screen. See Google's advertising information linked below.

Exports, backups, external services and support

When you export an Excel report or share a file, the selected file may contain detailed shop records. The file is written or shared only through the destination or receiving app you choose. If you choose a cloud drive, email app or another provider, that provider processes the file under its own policies.

Where your installed version includes full backup and restore, backups can contain the shop database, drafts, history and supported settings. Backups are not encrypted by default; use the password option when available and keep the password safe. Exported reports and files outside the App remain until you or the receiving provider remove them.

Some older versions allow Android system backup or device transfer. Newer versions may exclude App data from those mechanisms and use an explicit backup workflow. The actual behavior depends on your installed version, device and system settings. A locally stored record may therefore also exist in a device or cloud backup.

If you contact testfornewin1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message or files you choose to send. These are used to respond and investigate the request. Do not send passwords, full card details or raw purchase tokens. Google Play also processes information when you use its update, purchase or subscription-management features.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Shop data normally remains on your device until removed through the App or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. Some delete, cancel, refund or archive operations retain historical and audit records rather than erase every related entry. Versions with data-recovery safeguards may retain private upgrade or restore snapshots. Clearing the App's storage can remove local records and recovery copies, so first export anything you need to keep.

Deleting local data does not delete files you exported, backups managed by Android or another provider, subscription records held by Google or our backend, or messages you sent to support. Manage external copies with the relevant service.

We retain subscription and support information as needed to operate and verify subscriptions, handle requests and disputes, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Subscription records do not automatically disappear when the App is uninstalled or a subscription ends. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, or to ask about retention. We may need proportionate information to verify your request and may need to keep records required for an active entitlement, security, disputes or law. Google applies its own retention rules to its services.

We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a shop database stored only on your device. Rights and available choices depend on applicable law. To exercise them, use the privacy contact below.

Security, permissions and children

The App uses Android's app-private storage for its database and settings and HTTPS for subscription verification. Internet and network-state access support ads, subscription verification and Play services; file export and restore use Android's sharing or document-selection interfaces. The App does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise-location permissions.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device, restrict access to shop records and keep exported files and backups secure. Providers may process data in countries other than your own under their applicable policies and safeguards.

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess and address the request.

Providers, policy updates and contact

  • Google advertising information and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures explain advertising processing.
  • Firebase privacy and security explains the infrastructure used for subscription verification and storage.
  • GitHub Privacy Statement applies to the GitHub Pages service hosting this policy. Visiting the page may result in technical access logs, including your IP address. This policy page adds no advertising or analytics script.

We may update this policy when the App or its data handling changes. The effective and last-updated date appears at the top. We will provide any additional notice or choice required by applicable law.

適用範圍

本隱私權政策適用於 Natural Intelligence Studio 開發的 Small Shop POS System，繁體中文商店名稱為小店點餐系統，Android 套件名稱為 gundon.gu.posystem。App 在裝置上也可能顯示為「點餐管理系統」，以上名稱均指同一款平板 POS 應用程式。

本 App 協助店家管理商品、訂單、庫存、會計及報表，不要求另外註冊開發者帳號。訂閱所使用的 Google 帳號由 Google Play 管理。功能及備份行為可能依安裝版本而有所不同。

裝置上的營運資料

App 會將營運資料保存在本機資料庫及設定中，包括商品、配方、分類、售價、庫存與成本、訂單與客製化內容、桌號及備註、銷售與會計紀錄、收付款金額與分類、報表、語言與幣別、試用狀態及訂閱權益資訊。具備草稿、退款或稽核功能的版本，也會保存對應紀錄與歷程。

這些資料用於 POS 操作及報表產生。App 不會自動將營運資料庫上傳至訂閱驗證後端，也不會將其內容附加為廣告參數。現金、刷卡或行動支付等分類由店家自行記錄；本 POS 不處理顧客刷卡交易，也不要求輸入顧客卡號。

您在備註等自由文字欄位輸入的資訊會成為營運紀錄的一部分。請避免輸入非必要的顧客或員工個資、敏感資訊，並依您應遵守的規範處理所輸入資料。

訂閱與購買驗證

購買、恢復或查詢訂閱時，付款及購買流程由 Google Play 處理。App 會透過 HTTPS，將套件名稱、訂閱商品識別碼、購買憑證（purchase token），以及適用時的方案識別碼，傳送至我們使用 Google Firebase／Google Cloud Functions 建置的後端，再透過 Google Play Developer API 驗證權益。

後端會在 Cloud Firestore 保存訂閱驗證紀錄，可能包括購買憑證、商品與方案、訂閱狀態、開始與到期時間、自動續訂及確認狀態、關聯購買資訊、驗證時間及通知歷程。若驗證時有提供或 Google Play 回傳，紀錄也可能包含裝置或經模糊化的帳號識別碼。Android App不會隨這些請求傳送店家的營運資料庫。

這些資訊用於確認購買、恢復權益、處理續訂與取消、防止濫用及排查訂閱問題。Google 及其基礎設施供應商也可能處理 IP 位址、技術請求或服務日誌。我們不會從 Google Play 取得完整付款卡資料。刪除 App 或本機資料不會取消訂閱，請到 Google Play 管理取消。

廣告服務

含廣告的發行版本使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）。SDK 可能蒐集及分享 IP 位址、裝置與廣告識別碼、由 IP 推估的概略位置、App 與廣告互動，以及診斷或效能資訊。Google 會將這些資料用於廣告投放、依法允許時的個人化、成效衡量、防詐及服務運作。

廣告資料處理與本機 POS 營運資料分開。App 不會將訂單、庫存、營收、會計備註或顧客資訊附加到廣告請求。有效訂閱權益可隱藏 App 的橫幅廣告。

您可在 Android 或 Google 帳號管理可用的廣告控制項；實際選項依裝置、地區及設定而異。本政策不表示 App 目前已提供內建同意視窗或廣告隱私選項頁面。Google 廣告資料處理說明請見下方連結。
