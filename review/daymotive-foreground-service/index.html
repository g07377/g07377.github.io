Service context

The demonstration concerns FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE for a workout initiated by the user with training notifications enabled. The ongoing notification and optional floating shortcut provide access to the current workout while another app is in use.

This is supporting demonstration material, not evidence of Google Play approval. It does not demonstrate an audible alert or validate behavior on every device.
